## CPU reference baselines

Locally executed `scripts/evaluate_heuristic_baselines.py` on the repository train/valid files whose hashes were previously matched against the training backup. Raw results and hashes are in `HEURISTIC_VALID_20261021.json`.

1. `train_target_popularity`: rank SIDs by train target occurrence count; break ties by SID string; return the same Top-10 for every query.
2. `last_item_then_popularity`: put the last observed history SID first and fill with train-popular SIDs, avoiding duplicate candidates. This uses past history only, not validation targets, to construct candidates.

Both use the same scorer, target set, SID catalog, K values and no history exclusion. They are references for the current SFT, not GRPO or algorithm-optimization results.

| Last item then popularity | 0.075243 | 0.082966 | 0.068946 | 0.071407 |
| Current SFT | 0.038173 | 0.055384 | 0.035078 | 0.040476 |

Valid has 269 last-history SID matches but only 205 last-history raw-item matches, further demonstrating that SID recurrence and exact item recurrence are not interchangeable.

## Conclusion and next action

The engineering pipeline works and the full evaluation is recorded. Current aggregate SFT scores do not surpass these simple references; no positive improvement claim is supported. No paired significance test has been run, and training/generalization causes cannot be identified from aggregate metrics alone.

Before allocating more training to GRPO, inspect the saved per-query predictions for candidate concentration, prompt/target alignment and recurrence vs novel-target subgroups. Recompute metrics from those predictions and review examples. Keep this model and protocol as the initial E1 checkpoint; decide whether a controlled SFT adjustment is justified by that analysis. Original GRPO target-mapping fixes remain outstanding.


## Per-query audit (2026-10-21)

Executed `scripts/audit_predictions.py` on the user-supplied predictions. Reproducible output: `E1_VALID_FULL_20261021_audit.json`.

- All 4,532 rows have unique source indices and match the local valid CSV user, target item, target SID and complete history prompt. Full valid coverage; metrics reproduce exactly.
- Across all 45,320 Top-10 positions, only **28 distinct SIDs** are recommended out of 3,670 catalog SIDs (0.763% coverage).
- Only **4 distinct SIDs** appear at rank 1. `<a_107><b_93><c_0>` is first for 2,782 queries (61.386%). There are 418 distinct ordered Top-10 lists, so predictions are not literally identical for every history.
- Targets previously seen as raw items in history: 236 queries, SID HR@10 = 0.364407. Targets not seen in their own history: 4,296 queries, SID HR@10 = 0.038408. The latter does not mean globally cold-start items.

Observed conclusion: strong candidate concentration and weak recommendation quality. Zero invalid SIDs alone cannot establish that all catalog paths are reachable. Alignment checks rule out the checked row/prompt mismatch, not every possible data or evaluation defect.

Next diagnostic order: (1) verify that the real tokenizer/trie makes every catalog SID reachable; (2) compare selected backbone weights between the original base and exported SFT checkpoint and inspect parameter/optimizer dtypes; (3) inspect per-task learning and only then choose a controlled SFT change. BF16 parameter updates with the current optimizer and small learning rate are a hypothesis to investigate, not an established cause. No additional training or GRPO was launched during this audit.
